## What this settles and what it leaves

1. **The level outside January is a small, regime-dependent number** (0.66 log points a year in the pattern; realised −3 to +5.5 by year). Under a rule that requires every era, no fixed level passes, and the specification's expectation that the January-only rule would was wrong by the width of one cell.
2. **The block's error is the announcement coverage, not the level.** January 2023 alone is 30.9 log points; the announcement gate could not fire because the ledger row's availability is tied to the December index release. The primary documents cited in that row (the government decree of 5 October 2022, the MPO release of 23 June 2022, the ERU price decision of 14 November 2022) predate the November- and December-2022 origins' clocks (11 December 2022 and 10 January 2023). A ledger row for January 2023 available from 14 November 2022, priced off the October index and the decree's cap, would let those two origins carry the reversal. This is a documentation decision on the nowcast lane's ledger (hash-frozen data, hand-maintained) and is left to the user; the block scores in 2022–23 would change materially if it were taken, and every earlier round's administered numbers with it.
3. **From 2024 the case for dropping the drift is strong and consistent** (30–38% of block RMSE, all of the headline bias), which is why the November 2026 regulator decisions are the next live test of this block, not another historical round.
