## What works now

`tools/recording/verified_nowcast.py` accepts an existing engine run and checks its receipt, saved input frames, code/static-input hashes, recorded runtime, independent point names, first-release calendar, clocks and contribution arithmetic. It copies the verified source bytes before sealing a new bundle, so subsequent changes to the original input files do not destroy that bundle's evidence. Receipt-listed optional outputs are retained too.

The primary is HARD_BASE; HARD_HALF and HARD_FULL are stored as independent comparisons. Point units are monthly CPI percent; contributions are percentage points. This wrapper does not add consensus or expectations to these three forecasts. It does not integrate CATEGORY_RAW or issue probabilities.

There are two explicit modes:

- **Replay:** uses a simulated historical decision date. Historical source availability remains a labelled assumption. Actual verification time is stored separately.
- **Prospective:** requires a live-capture receipt, positive readiness checks, current component/fuel history, and publication before the target's first release. The completion-age check is at archive handoff (120 seconds); the archive then has its own 120-second issue-to-publication limit. These are two clocks, not a claim of a single 120-second completion-to-publication deadline.

The archive is a local integrity mechanism. It is not an external timestamp service, proof of original publication vintages, or proof that all upstream data loaders refreshed successfully.

## ERU energy source

Five official files are saved under `data/research_r19/eru_offers_20260915_complete/`: electricity and gas CSVs, the overview and methodology pages, and the methodology PDF. Each download has a retrieval timestamp and SHA256 in `source_manifest.json`.

There are 330 electricity rows (10 tariffs) and 99 gas rows (3 consumption segments), covering November 2025–September 2026 and 12/24/36-month fixed-price products. All keys are unique; bounds are finite and ordered; the inclusive-VAT columns equal the exclusive-VAT columns times 1.21 within numerical tolerance. The normalized copy retains the raw source price unit because the CSV headers do not explicitly certify the monetary/unit denominator. Confirm that display unit before feeding a bill calculator.

These are modelled commercial offer bands, excluding regulated charges. They are neither a national average price paid nor statistical forecast intervals. Contract length describes the product being offered, not the remaining maturity of household contracts. Sources: [ERU overview](https://eru.gov.cz/ipnc), [methodology](https://eru.gov.cz/metodika-stanoveni-ipnc).

The downloaded methodology's section 11 specifies publication on the first working day of the month. Section 8.6 also declares a change from cumulative-average offer margins to a twelve-month rolling average after September 2026. This is a source-method change to track. These publication rules do not prove when the historical CSV rows were first accessible: the current file contains months beyond its web attachment's July update label. Accordingly, all normalized rows carry the actual download time as `available_from`, and historical first-release verification is false. None enter an earlier backtest origin.

Use this source to investigate replacement-offer conditions. To calculate a national CPI effect we still need household exposure/renewal weights, existing contract prices, regulated charges, fixed fees and a verified unit mapping. No weights have been guessed, no national contribution inferred, and no improvement claim made from eleven months of data.
