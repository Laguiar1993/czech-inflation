READING THE EVIDENCE

Keep these distinctions visible

  • Flash ≠ full detail. Each panel carries its own month.
  • Contribution ≠ acceleration. The driver selector answers either question.
  • Correlation ≠ pass-through. Pipeline relationships are descriptive and sample-dependent.
  • Rebased ≠ re-estimated. Substituting actual prints does not update future monthly forecasts.
  • Model spread ≠ uncertainty band. No probability interpretation is assigned to the model range.
  • Historical replay ≠ prospective record. Forecasts are reconstructed at historical information clocks.
