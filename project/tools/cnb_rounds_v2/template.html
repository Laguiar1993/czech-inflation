CNB Rounds Replayed

Each panel freezes one CNB report. The red points are the bank's forecast of quarterly average inflation, out to the last quarter in that report. The coloured lines are our monthly paths replayed from a simulated cutoff before the report, with current-vintage inputs and no knowledge of the report. The dark line is what happened. Below each headline chart, the same round is shown block by block, and the gap to CNB is split into the blocks that carry it.
