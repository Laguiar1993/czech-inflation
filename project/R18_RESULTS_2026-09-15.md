# CZK Cpi Forecasting - Latest models

## R18 assessment and decision

The six-part improvement programme has been implemented and evaluated. It improves the data, diagnostics, scenario accounting and auditability of the system. It does **not** establish a replacement for the existing independent nowcast or a consistently better inflation path.

The new category model can produce economically interpretable turns that the simpler trend models miss. A version with hard economic signals improves some short-horizon and CNB comparisons. However, both versions deteriorate materially at twelve months in the recent period. The food update adds little. The new probability filters do not turn FULL's disagreements with consensus into reliable advance alerts. The simple historical path-error bands fail calibration.

Keep the existing point-forecast roster. Retain the category model as a research diagnostic, and retain the household-energy calculator as explicitly conditional scenarios. Do not deploy the new probability gate or label the path bands calibrated.

## 2. The new category models

The data add ten goods groups, two housing groups and six services groups. They include rents, household equipment-related goods, vehicle purchases, information/communication equipment and services, recreation, catering and accommodation. The separately measured CNB core series is another measurement. These categories are **not an exact partition of CNB core**: they differ in coverage and tax treatment, and the current recoded historical classification is not an archived contemporary taxonomy.

At each origin, the model uses 48–96 already published monthly observations. It estimates calendar-month means and robust observation-noise scales from that origin's training history. After removing those calendar means, each series is represented by:

**common persistent pressure + common temporary pressure + its own persistent component + measurement noise.**

The common persistent state follows a random walk. The temporary state retains 30% of its previous value each month; the sector state retains 95%. The two fixed versions change only the innovation variance of the common persistent state. The forecast adds destination-month seasonality back. Because the last observation is t−1, a prediction for t+1 requires two state transitions; that alignment is tested.

This construction asks whether price pressure is broad or isolated. An isolated jump in accommodation should mainly affect its sector component; coordinated movements across categories should influence common pressure. The framework is economically interpretable, but its statistical weighting is based on measurement precision, not official CPI expenditure shares. Correlation among the categories and unequal economic coverage can give some groups too much influence.

The third version adds eight hard-data features: unemployment change, industrial-production growth, unit-labour-cost growth, FX change, two imported-cost summaries, category breadth and the goods/services state gap. Twelve direct regressions share a penalty against abrupt changes in coefficients across adjacent horizons. Training uses only saved earlier forecasts whose target outcomes have been published. With insufficient history, the correction is zero. It is fitted at 54 of the 90 origins. There is no search across a large grid after seeing these results.

The signal model has a noteworthy squared-error result. Even omitting the February 2022 report, its RMSE is 1.430 versus CNB 1.476. However, on that same omission its MAE is 0.979 versus CNB 0.704. Its full information-cutoff MAE is 1.363 versus CNB 1.061, and its recent report-clock RMSE is 0.604 versus CNB 0.372. This is a model that sometimes reduces a large error while doing worse on many ordinary forecasts; it is not demonstrated CNB dominance.

The strict underlying-core turn diagnostic has 159 overlapping opportunities and 77 actual turns. The fast category model makes 42 calls: 22 exact-band hits and 20 false calls, missing 55 turns. Its precision is 52.4% and recall 28.6%; the signal version has 20 hits and 19 false calls. Existing simple trend models make no qualifying interior-turn calls under this definition.

That is a diagnostic advance, but **22 is not a count of turns spotted before CNB**. It is not even 22 independent episodes. The comparison uses the same saved origin-specific seasonal benchmark as earlier rounds. On the separate sustained-movement test, the fast category model has only 6 hits from 22 calls; the signal version has 8 from 39. We still cannot defend a repeatable advance turning-point advantage over CNB.

## 5. Nowcast accuracy and your large-surprise objective

The four point forecasts are unchanged. They remain release-eve forecasts of the first published monthly CPI change: ordinary first release before the flash regime, flash against flash thereafter. Consensus is the benchmark for that same release, not an input into these point forecasts.

| Point forecast | RMSE, all 90 | RMSE, 2024+ (31) | Material wins / losses on 23 big surprises |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Consensus | 0.382 | 0.241 | — |
| BASE | 0.418 | 0.219 | 7 / 3 |
| HALF | 0.414 | 0.220 | 7 / 1 |
| FULL | 0.414 | 0.225 | 9 / 2 |
| Category Raw | 0.409 | 0.198 | 8 / 2 |

A big surprise is at least 0.4 pp actual-minus-consensus in absolute value. A material gain requires the model's absolute error to be at least 0.15 pp smaller than consensus's. A matching direction alone does not qualify; excessive overshoots can lose.

FULL gets the direction right on 17 of those 23 big releases, with 9 material wins and 2 material losses. Its conditional MAE is 0.476 versus consensus 0.578. But we learn that a release was big only afterward. When FULL actually deviates from consensus by at least 0.2 pp, its 23 calls include only 7 big surprises. Across all those calls its MAE is 0.321 versus consensus 0.265. This gap between conditional capture and advance identification remains central to your goal.

R18 tests whether past errors can identify more reliable calls. It uses at most 60 eligible previous release errors and needs at least 24, leaving 66 scored origins. The three laws are signed pooled errors, volatility-scaled/recent-weighted errors, and a small additional weighting toward similar BASE/FULL/category disagreements. None changes the independent point forecast.

The prespecified gate requires at least a 65% estimated probability of a material gain as well as a 0.2 pp model/consensus gap. For FULL:

| Rule, same 66 eligible dates | Calls | Actual big surprises | Material wins / losses | Model MAE | Consensus MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gap only | 18 | 6 | 7 / 10 | 0.373 | 0.278 |
| Pooled probability gate | 9 | 4 | 3 / 6 | 0.460 | 0.311 |
| Scaled probability gate | 8 | 4 | 3 / 5 | 0.468 | 0.338 |
| Disagreement-conditioned gate | 6 | 2 | 1 / 5 | 0.541 | 0.267 |

These filters fail their purpose. Do not call the estimated probabilities calibrated or use the threshold automatically for position sizing. The original points remain competitive in the recent period; the new probability layer does not prove an exploitable signal. HALF remains a legitimate conservative challenger, not an inferior model by definition.

## 6. Food, energy and uncertainty

The food experiment takes the saved independent food nowcast's difference from the food state model and tests whether that news persists. Fast/slow profiles and a sequential selector are estimated using matured previous paths. The standalone food gains are tiny and recent headline accuracy worsens. Retain this as a negative result rather than importing the news mechanically into twelve future months. [Food report](work/research_r18_food/R18_FOOD_RESULTS_2026-09-15.md).

The energy work finds a concrete source route: ERU's new retail-monitoring forms contain contract types, quantities, prices and regulated/commercial revenue fields. The public schemas do not supply individual monthly reset dates, and this audit did not locate a populated national exposure extract. Original duration buckets are not the remaining reset schedule; customer counts are not CPI expenditure weights.

The implemented calculator handles reset cohorts, fixed-consumption bills, persistent price levels, missing population mass and policy/baseline replacement. It produces 60 explicitly conditional electricity rows across five assumed portfolios. All national point-forecast and headline-impact fields remain empty. Do not fit a January multiplier to fill those gaps. The next useful acquisition is dated supplier/product expenditure weights and the joint distribution of remaining resets, agreed price steps and bills. [Energy findings and official sources](work/research_r18_energy/R18_ENERGY_RESULTS_2026-09-15.md).

The path-band experiment preserves historical cross-month error dependence: it resamples whole h0–h12 error vectors, only after all outcomes are published. Each sampled monthly path compounds coherently into annual CPI. It does not pretend independently selected horizon quantiles form one coherent scenario.

The simple empirical distribution fails calibration. FAST's nominal 90% h12 band covers 26/39 full-sample outcomes (66.7%) despite an average width of 15.94 pp. In the recent period it covers 19/19, with average width 16.81 pp. Before a regime break the available error history understates the new shock; afterward old crisis errors dominate normal-condition bands. Coherent arithmetic is necessary but does not make uncertainty economically useful. These are historical error sensitivities, not operational probability bands or probabilities assigned to a new war.

The final uncertainty scoreboard uses the original 969 intended keys, then a common eligible distribution calendar across all models. It explicitly reports intended, missing and scored counts; h12 has only 39 common estimated/realised paths. ## 7. Reliability improvements and remaining operational work

The new archive API creates exclusive UUID bundles, hashes actual code/input/manifest files, records issue and source-availability times, and separates replay from prospective mode. Prospective mode checks the actual wall clock before the first release, preventing a historical replay from acquiring a live label. A commit seal detects incomplete or changed payloads. It is a local integrity contract, not a trusted external timestamp or an immutable filesystem. Source availability remains a declaration whose truth needs real acquisition evidence. No live forecast was fabricated to test it. [Archive contract](docs/implementation/R18_ARCHIVE_SPEC_2026-09-15.md).

The revision report reconciles changes in core, food, administered prices, alcohol/tobacco, fuel and wedge contributions to the **monthly** forecast revision. Annual-rate revisions are reported separately: they also depend on compounding and the changing known base. Seven inherited unavailable comparisons remain explicitly unavailable. [Revision ledger](output/research_r18/path_v2/monthly_component_revisions.csv).

Before this becomes a dependable desk process, connect the archive to the real data-refresh/forecast command, retain each newly observed source vintage, add failed/stale-input reporting, and acquire the missing energy exposures. The archive API and research outputs delivered here are not a completed unattended refresh service or a newly validated trading strategy.

## 8. Corrections and verification

Independent reviews corrected availability-failure handling and exact FAST fallback copying in the category runner; fitted-history counts now distinguish the 96-observation fit cap from total available history. The full 90-origin forecasts and saved states are unchanged. The evaluation preserves unavailable component rows and original model identifiers in the replay.

The original empirical-quantile implementation could select the next atom at exact probability boundaries because of floating-point accumulation. Corrected, independently checked quantiles changed 280 nowcast median/band cells and two 80% coverage flags; all points, laws, probabilities, CRPS and alerts stayed exact. A separate positive-tail p=1 edge was corrected. Both model callers cap support at 60 observations; the helper is not certified as an arbitrary-size quantile library. [Correction and parity note](work/research_r18_nowcast_review/QUANTILE_CORRECTION_NOTE.md).

**523 targeted and regression tests pass.** The first broader collection lacked access to the already-recorded DuckDB runtime; rerunning offline with that dependency path passed. No dependency, ACL or old forecasting source was changed. The 14 earlier R15–R17 manifests still verify, covering 1,623 input/output hash entries. Final checks independently reconstruct the category filter/regression, food coefficients, 3,300 primary score rows, CNB comparisons, 405,210 joint error coordinates and 7,092 interval rows. The replay's 19 rounds under each clock, model switches, tooltips and finite SVG geometry pass DOM-contract tests; this is not a pixel-level browser-layout certification.

My assessment is that the existing independent forecasts remain useful for cross-checking the market and explaining component risks. R18 makes the research more defensible and adds a promising category-turn diagnostic, but it does not justify stronger claims about overall accuracy, advance surprise detection or reliable CNB outperformance. The next improvement should come from better identified component news and recorded prospective evidence, with category breadth tested as a diagnostic or restrained response-speed input rather than automatically replacing the whole core path.
