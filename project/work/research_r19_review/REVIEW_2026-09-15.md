Two reproducible findings remain in the reviewed adapter:

1. [P2] The completion freshness check does not cover archive publication. tools/recording/verified_nowcast.py:135-148 checks engine completion against the post-staging observed clock, then passes issued_at=observed to the archive. The archive enforces its own 120-second issue-to-publication window, but does not know the earlier engine completion clock. In test_archiver_delay_over_completion_limit_is_rejected, the engine completed 118 seconds before verification; advancing the clock by 3 seconds inside archive artifact hashing still produces a committed prospective bundle with completion age 121 seconds. If the intended requirement is completion within 120 seconds at publication, enforce that deadline at the final publication gate. A post-commit error alone is insufficient because COMMITTED.json would already exist. If freshness is intentionally only a handoff-time guarantee, the operational contract must state that narrower guarantee explicitly.

2. [P2] Receipt-listed optional artifacts are verified but discarded from the retained archive. tools/recording/verified_nowcast.py:103 hardcodes snapshot.json, receipt.json and forecast.json as source manifests, although the initial receipt verification accepts and checks every receipt item. A legitimate forecast_independent.py --path run also receipts path.json and path.csv. test_all_verified_receipt_artifacts_are_bound confirms both are absent from source_path_map and archive artifact groups. The sealed bundle can pass verify_artifacts=True after those original path outputs disappear, despite its retained receipt referring to them. Copy all verified receipt items as source artifacts while continuing to leave path_forecasts empty and preserving the explicit unsupported-current-path label.
