# CZK Cpi Forecasting - Latest models

R12 research results, 9 September 2026. Seven fixed numerical variants were tested:
three category equations and four inflation paths. The all-month energy wrapper was
also built and evaluated for historical eligibility. Online retailer collection was
excluded as requested. No operating model was replaced.

## Decision

Retain BASE as the main independent next-release nowcast, the original category
model as the research accuracy challenger, and HALF/FULL as the existing correction
comparisons. Retain the independent component bridge as the path reference. The new
pooling result is a small research improvement in headline error, with qualifications;
the new path variants do not earn promotion. The energy mechanism is implemented,
but the historical inputs do not support a complete strict addition at any origin.

This round implements the first three priorities and a separate monthly-versus-
cumulative path experiment. It does not fit another BBIM or dynamic-quantile forest.
The existing independent forest and BVAR remain path comparators. The results below
provide a more specific next question before another nonlinear model is introduced.

## Nowcast: pooling helps a little, but not enough to reorganise the roster

All errors here are percentage points of monthly headline inflation. There are 90
matching first releases (February 2019–July 2026), 31 recent releases from 2024,
and 23 large surprises. Large means |actual-consensus| >=0.4pp; a material win/loss
means reducing/increasing absolute error against consensus by at least 0.15pp.
Targets are the ordinary first release before 2025 and the flash from January 2025.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

The six equations retain their own seasonal patterns. Their persistence
coefficients are pulled towards common values; lagged aggregate core is a shared
signal. The sixth target is a scaled statistical remainder, not an official goods
or services category. The same origin-available weights reconstruct all its past
observations. No expectations or release consensus enters the estimators.

The moderate version improves headline RMSE by about 0.6% against original categories,
and by about 2.7% against BASE. But most of the additional gain appears in the
separate-equation control after adding the common core lag: actual pooling contributes
only another 0.00068pp reduction in RMSE. Its recent RMSE is slightly worse than
original categories. Full-window paired uncertainty against BASE still crosses no
improvement, and the many historical comparisons are not selection-adjusted.

More importantly, moderate pooling slightly worsens core RMSE (0.2481 versus 0.2465
for original categories). Its headline MSE gain of 0.001968pp² against categories
decomposes into a **negative** weighted-core gain of -0.000218pp² plus favourable
core/non-core error offsets of +0.002187pp². This is not evidence of a cleaner core
signal. At recent origins core RMSE also worsens (0.1565 versus 0.1416).

Pooling recovers two lost direction calls relative to original categories, but
still trails BASE/FULL on large-surprise MAE. All-release alerts and material losses
remain in `pooling/release_rows.csv` and `scores.csv`; conditional big-event success
is not advance knowledge of which releases will surprise. No post-result blend or
correction was fitted. HALF remains half of FULL's own baseline-error correction,
not a BASE/category blend, and that correction was not transplanted onto pooling.

## Energy: operational accounting, no defensible historical gain yet

The new wrapper can process effective months throughout the year, including linked
starts and expiries. It checks evidence dates for bills, household exposure, CPI
treatment, basket weights and the baseline contribution being replaced. Unknown
effects return unavailable; they are not silently estimated as zero.

The integration retains January's existing comparator because its overlapping
treatment would require an item-level baseline decomposition to replace safely.
For other months, a complete strict event payload is required before an addition.
There are **zero qualifying additions and zero changed forecasts** in this sample.
The 90 administered forecasts and solved weights reproduce exactly. All 60 previous
monetary scenarios also reproduce, without selecting or scoring a best scenario.

The evidence gap is concrete: dated representative bills, customer contract shares,
the saving-credit CPI expenditure/quantity base, and the energy seasonality already
embedded in the baseline are missing. A supplier announcement alone does not supply
them. Newly inspected CZSO methodology explains the treatment but does not establish
availability before the corresponding release-eve cutoff. Detailed sources and the
five minimum data requirements are in `energy/NOTES.md` and its source inventory.

This is useful infrastructure and a defined collection task, not a new validated
forecaster. It also does not retrospectively certify BASE's January reconstruction.

## Path: lower recent bias does not imply a better model

The two food experiments replace only h4–h12 food. They keep h0–h3 and all other
blocks unchanged. One separates a centred calendar pattern from an evolving trend.
The other adds agricultural and food-producer-price signals, with conservative
source lags and a correction that decays at longer horizons. Source-month mappings
were checked against the raw caches; these are reconstructed publication rules,
not archived historical vintages.

The other pair compares individual monthly targets with average cumulative log
price changes, using identical simple headline-state predictors and shrinkage.
Both preserve BASE at h0. All annual paths compound exactly, and h12 training
waits for the entire future target window to have been released.

| Annual-inflation RMSE | Full h6, N81 | Full h12, N75 | Recent-target h12, N31 | Recent-origin h12, N19 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Existing component bridge | 2.516 | 5.395 | 1.892 | 1.347 |
| Food trend and seasonality | 2.574 | 5.691 | 2.342 | 1.313 |
| Food trend plus upstream costs | 2.583 | 5.715 | 2.354 | 1.320 |
| Direct monthly headline model | 3.160 | 6.588 | 5.800 | 1.252 |
| Direct cumulative headline model | 3.163 | 6.563 | 5.733 | 1.254 |
| Last observed annual inflation held flat | 4.227 | 6.969 | 6.222 | 1.175 |

Recent-target means the forecast destination is January 2024 or later; recent-origin
means the forecast itself was made for January 2024 or later. They answer different
questions. At h12 only 19 recent-origin outcomes are realised, versus 31 recent
targets that include forecasts made during 2023. All panels must remain visible.

The food-trend change reduces recent-origin h12 bias from +0.730pp to +0.113pp,
but barely improves RMSE and slightly worsens MAE. It loses over the full sample
and recent-target panel. The upstream-cost extension is marginally worse again.
All recent-origin h12 paired error intervals cross no improvement. A full-window
12-month block interval instead indicates deterioration for both food variants.

The direct cumulative model marginally improves full h12 RMSE against its matched
monthly control. Both are substantially worse than the component bridge. The 2023
origin group is especially revealing: cumulative-model h12 bias is +8.225pp,
compared with the bridge's +1.893pp. A recent aggregate trend can carry temporary
headline shocks forward too persistently. This interpretation is consistent with
the errors; it is not a causal identification of every miss.

These results reject these particular implementations for promotion. They do not
establish that all evolving-trend models or cumulative targets are ineffective.
There was no search for a more favourable smoothing span, penalty or sample after
seeing these scores. Existing independent forest/BVAR and publication-matched CNB
comparison tables remain available in the full path output.

## What to use and the next focused experiment

Keep the existing roster stable. The next path hypothesis should retain the economic
component separation and ask whether cumulative **core** inflation can be forecast
better with shrinkage and properly lagged cost/labour information. Model temporary
administered/tax changes separately so they do not become persistent underlying
inflation. This is a new prospective experiment to specify, not an unreported model
already fitted here. A richer blockwise nonlinear challenger can then be judged
against the same component bridge and simple benchmarks.

For the nowcast, complete the dated energy input mapping and integrate the existing
category challenger into timestamped shadow output before expanding the operating
display. The pooling candidates remain preserved research diagnostics. For both
products, protect a prospective evaluation record: the historical sample has been
inspected repeatedly and cannot provide fresh holdout evidence.

## Reproduction and audit

Declarations were committed before fits (`cccb3ca` pooling; `abd1342` energy/path).
All old numerical models, inputs and saved results are preserved. Timing, future
poisoning, accounting and scoring tests accompany the new estimators. Independent
review found and corrected constant-series floating-point scaling in the path
ridge helper and an active-log manifest hash issue. The source-date reconstruction
now also applies calendar arithmetic before timezone localisation, preserving the
intended publication date across daylight-saving changes. The energy runner handles
portable folders without Git. These corrections do not change the empirical
forecasts; they protect edge cases and reproducibility. The final verification receipt
records test totals, output replays and the portable package checks.

Use the package's START_HERE.md for commands. `core_pooling_experiment_r12.py --verify`
re-estimates all 90 pooling origins offline. The path runner's `--verify` checks and
re-scores saved predictions; a fresh path run reproduces the actual fitted forecasts.
The energy runner replays accounting and eligibility without fitting parameters.
The supplied scientific runtime lock is unchanged; live input refresh and genuine
historical vintages remain separate operational requirements.
