# R17 food cost transmission: frozen child specification

Prepared 14 September 2026 before any R17 food fitted outcomes. This is exploratory
research after the negative R14/R14B/R16 evidence, not a fresh holdout or causal
identification claim. The original source files, experiments and controls stay
immutable. No tuning or candidate additions follow the full run.

## Inputs and clocks

Use only the verified R14 `pipeline_log_levels.csv`,
`pipeline_available_from.csv`, their input manifest and its original dependencies.
The three variables are agri4, food_ppi and food; levels are 100 log points and
begin January 2015. These are current stored histories with reconstructed release
availability, not historical revision vintages. Monthly rates are differences of
adjacent calendar levels, eligible only if both timestamps are known and no later
than the decision clock, and both reference months are strictly before origin t.
Never fill a missing endpoint or collapse calendar gaps. No future cost levels or
model-imputed costs enter predictors. Unknown availability is unavailable.

Read decision timestamps from immutable R15 states. Interpret naive timestamps in
Europe/Prague and verify all outer clocks equal R16 FAST clocks. Freeze snapshots
independently for each month February 2016–July 2026 (126 months). The first date
permits all twelve food destination-month means using February 2015–January 2016.
The original 90 outer origins are February 2019–July 2026. h1 forecasts t+1 from
food observed no later than t−1, a two-month step; no h0 food estimate or current
food realization enters this experiment. HARD_BASE headline h0 is preserved.

## Origin-frozen features and controls

At each snapshot, use the last 96 calendar months of eligible food rates (all if
fewer), calculating a separate mean for each destination month. Every calendar
month requires at least one released observation; otherwise all forecasts remain
missing with `unavailable_seasonality`. Save means and underlying reference dates.
For food, subtract each lag's own month mean, then use the last one and mean of
last three contiguous residual rates: own1 and own3. If a required food lag is
unavailable, own features remain missing. Persistence equals destination-month
seasonality plus own3 at all h1..12. If own3 is unavailable, persistence explicitly
falls back to the seasonal mean, with a recorded reason.

Upstream predictors are mean raw monthly log changes in lags 1..3 and 4..6
relative to origin, separately for agri4 and food_ppi. Symmetric features use the
signed mean. Asymmetric features use mean(max(change,0)) and mean(min(change,0))
within each same window; the negative leg retains its negative sign. Their sum
equals the symmetric feature. Signs are taken before averaging; upstream series
are not seasonally centered, so the sign is an actual price increase/decrease.
Any missing constituent makes that window unavailable. Save six lag values,
reference dates and both endpoint release timestamps per series.

Fixed roster:

- FOOD_SEASONAL_R17: frozen destination-month mean.
- FOOD_PERSISTENCE_R17: mean plus frozen own3.
- FOOD_OWN_R17: direct small own-food ridge, two own predictors.
- FOOD_SYMMETRIC_R17: own predictors plus four symmetric cost predictors.
- FOOD_ASYMMETRIC_R17: own predictors plus eight sign-separated cost predictors.
- FOOD_HALF_CORRECTION_R17: persistence + 0.5*(asymmetric − persistence), in
  monthly log rates, followed by exact conversion to m/m percent.

## Direct training and fixed regularization

Estimate one direct equation per h=1..12, response food log rate at s+h minus the
seasonal mean frozen at historical origin s for that destination month. The
regressor is exactly snapshot s saved at its own historical decision clock. At
current origin t, every s+h must be strictly less than t and both response level
endpoints must have been released by t's clock. The same historical support is
used for own, symmetric and asymmetric equations: both own and every upstream
feature must be finite, even in the own-food control. Only eligible rows enter;
use latest 96 such historical origins, minimum 24 per equation. Save every
training origin, target, endpoint timestamp, sample size, scale and coefficient.
Do not rebuild an earlier feature or baseline with a later fit.

There is no intercept. Scale each regressor by its training-only root mean
square; if RMS <=1e-12, use scale 1. Divide the least-squares loss by n. Ridge
penalty on the two own coefficients is always 0.1; penalties on upstream
coefficients are lambda in {0.1, 1, 10}. All prior means are zero. No coefficient
sign constraints, winsorization, clipping, forecast bounds or selected lag lengths.
The same own control and same own penalties accompany both cost models.

Fit and save all three fixed lambda forecasts chronologically for each upstream
model. Choose a single lambda for its whole current h1..12 path. Selection uses
the latest 36 historical origins whose twelve labels are all strictly before t,
released by t's clock, and for which every fixed lambda path was actually fitted
without fallback. Loss is the average of twelve squared monthly log-rate errors
at each origin; then the average across origins. Require 12 complete origins,
otherwise use lambda=1 with `default_insufficient_mature_validation`. Resolve
loss ties by fixed preference 1, 10, 0.1. Half correction inherits the asymmetric
selection and adds no fit or search. Selection never uses current outcomes.

If an equation has fewer than 24 rows or any current required predictor is
missing, its forecast explicitly falls back to the frozen persistence control.
Each row records estimated/fallback/unavailable status and exact reason; no
origin, horizon or forecast is dropped to manufacture common support. Fallback
is part of the deployed experimental rule, and fallback-only support is reported
separately from fitted rows. This choice makes the short early history visible.
This is a direct lag-feature association forecast, not a complete projected
supply-chain mechanism.

## Output, integration and scoring

Convert log rate r to monthly percent as 100*expm1(r/100). Cumulative h1..h log
change is their sum and cumulative percent is 100*expm1(sum/100), requiring every
month. Standalone saved predictions include earlier origins for combination
validation. Main food outcomes retain the original 90 origins and compare the
six fixed models with the exact saved FAST food path on full, origins2024+ and
targets2024+ calendars for h1..12 and pooled h1..12. Show monthly, cumulative log
and cumulative percent RMSE, MAE, bias and explicit coverage/fallback counts.

Replace only FAST future food values and contributions with origin-specific
weights. Recompute total monthly headline from all six contributions with a
six-value requirement and exact annual compounding using unchanged known history
and HARD_BASE h0. All nonfood values/contributions/weights are bit-identical.
Preserve FAST source rows as a control. Save standalone paths, fixed candidate
paths, snapshots, fit/selection logs, outcomes, component scores, native forecasts,
validation and manifest under a new non-existing destination. Parent performs
headline/control-calendar evaluation, CNB clocks, uncertainty and combinations.

Before full execution write a source/parameter declaration containing SHA256 of
this specification, model, runner, tests, all inputs and both complete R15/R16
manifest graphs. Verify every declared earlier input and output hash before and
after execution. Save declaration before fitting and manifest outputs afterward.
Smoke run has a separate destination and exercises the same equations. Changes
needed to fix demonstrated implementation bugs are recorded; fitted outcomes do
not justify altering this specification.

Tests precede implementation: signed decomposition, both endpoint gates, future
poisoning, own-origin seasonality and train-only scales, contiguous lag failures,
horizon and publication maturity, fallback visibility, full-path chronological
lambda selection, exact half correction/compounding and preserved h0/nonfood.
