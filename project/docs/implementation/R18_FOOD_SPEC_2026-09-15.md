## Fixed generating equations

Let p[s,h] be the saved pipeline monthly food prediction in 100 log points and
q[s,0] = 100*log1p(food_pred_eve[s]/100). Save d[s]=q[s,0]-p[s,0].
Do not treat q and p as independent observations and do not update a state
covariance. No h0 actual enters current predictions.

Two fixed profiles: FAST rho=.5 and SLOW rho=.9. At h=1..12, x[s,h]=d[s]*rho^h.
For each profile, fit one coefficient for the entire current path to
e[s,h]=actual_food_log[s+h]-p[s,h]. Training uses the latest 60 saved origins
whose entire h1..12 path has matured, both endpoints have been released, and
signal/baseline/labels are finite. Require 12 complete origins. Each origin
has equal weight and each of its twelve monthly log-rate errors has equal
weight. No intercept, horizon-specific beta, selected lag, clipping of forecasts
or selected training window.

With n complete origins and N=12*n rows, r=sqrt(mean(x^2)) using training only.
When r<=1e-12 return beta=0. Otherwise z=x/r and minimize
mean((e-theta*z)^2) + 1*theta^2. Hence theta=sum(z*e)/(sum(z*z)+N).
Convert beta_unbounded=theta/r to original signal units BEFORE imposing
beta=min(1,max(0,beta_unbounded)). This is 50% ridge shrinkage of the unbounded
OLS beta because the training z RMS is one. Beta is dimensionless; theta is
100 log points. The fixed zero prior and beta bound prohibit extrapolating the
incremental h0 signal by more than one. Fewer than 12 origins => beta=0 with
explicit `fallback_insufficient_mature_origins`; missing current signal =>
unchanged control with `fallback_missing_h0_signal`.

Save FOOD_H0_FAST_R18 and FOOD_H0_SLOW_R18 monthly food log rates
p[t,h]+beta[t]*d[t]*rho^h. Convert by 100*expm1(rate/100). At beta=0 return
the exact saved control monthly value, avoiding a numerical log/exp round trip.

Also save FOOD_H0_SELECT_R18. It chooses one ENTIRE saved candidate path from
unchanged FAST, FAST-profile and SLOW-profile, using the latest 36 origins with
complete matured h1..12 labels and all three finite actually saved paths.
Loss is mean squared monthly food log error, equal weights across horizons and
origins. Require 12 complete origins; default/ties (absolute 1e-12) choose
unchanged FAST, then FAST profile, then SLOW profile. Fallback paths are valid
issued choices and remain explicitly flagged. No current outcome, intercept,
outer full-sample winner or cherry-picked month chooses a coefficient/path.
