# R27 results: a fixed level correction from the producer-price gap improves the food block at h1–6, and is promoted as a fixed rule, not as an estimator

Run and independent review on 18 September 2026; written 20 September 2026, Claude. **Two candidates, one declared run, one independent adversarial review. `FOOD_ECM_R27` passes every accuracy condition of the R26 rule under independent reproduction and replaces `FOOD_NORM_SHIFT_R24` as the research roster's food path, re-labelled**: it is a fixed correction that closes about 0.6 of the retail-to-producer price gap over six months, not the estimated error-correction speed the specification declared. ## What was done

At each of the 90 origins, from the three published food log-level series (farm prices, producer prices, retail food) and their publication stamps: the last month L at which all three are published (origin−1 at 90 of 90 origins); a window of at most 96 months ending at L; the residual of the retail level on a constant, a trend and the upstream levels (`FOOD_ECM_R27`: producer and farm; `FOOD_ECM_PPI_R27`: producer only), centred by calendar month; an adjustment speed α by least squares of the centred monthly food rate on the previous month's gap, clipped to [−0.25, 0]; the correction α·gap_L·(1+α)^steps added to the R24 food log rate at h1–6. Four fixed speeds (−0.02 to −0.15) were run for the specificity condition. Nothing else changes: h0, h7–12, every other block, the wedge and the weights are the R24 run's.

## What the review changed in the interpretation

1. **It is a fixed rule with one number.** The forecasts on the roster are the run's `FOOD_ECM_R27` series, which equal a fixed speed of −0.25 at every origin; they are carried as "subtract 0.25 of the last gap, decaying at 0.75 a month, over h1–6" (closing 0.62 of the gap). The claim that an adjustment speed is estimated is withdrawn. Any speed from −0.20 to −0.40 gives the same score.
2. **It is not error correction with those dynamics.** The reviewer regressed the baseline's monthly food error on the gap across origins (`probe_06_double_counting/`): the response is right-sized at h1 (−0.173 against −0.188 applied) and then **grows** to −0.47 at h6 and persists at −0.24 to −0.42 through h12 with nothing applied; cumulatively the baseline error loads −1.92 on the gap at h6 (3.1 times what is applied) and −3.71 at h12. What the candidate captures is the sign and a fraction of a slow signal. There is no double counting: FAST's own h1 error responds to the gap exactly as the R24 path's does, so the rate VAR carries no level information. A persistent form (the gap applied through h12, with a declared shape) is the next declaration for this block; it is not adopted here because it was seen on outcomes.
3. **The relation is not the specification's equilibrium.** The own-level control (retail level on constant and trend, no upstream regressor) is worse than the baseline in nine of twelve cells (era-balanced 1.033, raw speed −0.03): the producer-price level carries the signal, which is the one part of the story that survives. The coefficients are unstable and the farm price is second order; `FOOD_ECM_PPI_R27` is carried as an equivalent alternative, and the farm version is on the roster only because of the 2024+ cells.
4. **Tests.** Three of the reviewer's sixteen mutants survived the original suite (clip at −0.5, gap taken at L−1, uncentred gap returned), one of which changes the promoted number. Three tests were added; all sixteen mutants are now killed (`work/research_r27_review_recheck/recheck_results.txt`).

Limits the reviewer put on the size of the gain, all adopted: 2021–22 origins carry 39% of the h6 and 50% of the h12 squared-loss gain, but without them the ratios are 0.825 and 0.841, and without the five largest gains 0.937 and 0.957 (leave-one-year-out worst 0.949 and 0.963); half of the h6 gain from 2024 and two thirds of the h12 gain from 2024 exist only with the R24 drift in place (on FAST's own food block the ratios are 0.897 and 0.957), so the 10–17% from 2024 is a joint result with R24; the correction worsens the block bias in every era (h6 full −0.93 → −1.14 log points) and the whole gain is variance; the CNB lead test is unchanged in verdict (17 mature first calls, 4 gains, 10 losses, 3 joint successes, against the baseline's 19/6/12/3) and from 2024 the candidate has no material gain where the baseline had two.

## Amendments to the rule, for the next specification

- **Condition 3's grid must bracket the estimate.** A grid that stops before the estimator's value cannot test specificity; the grid extends until the score turns, and the estimator is compared with the best member found that way.
- **The interval criterion needs materiality.** "Excludes zero" on a squared-loss difference of +0.003 says nothing; the amendment asks for the interval to exclude zero *and* the point estimate to be at least 1% of the reference RMSE.
- **A candidate that never leaves a bound is a constant** and is declared as one.
