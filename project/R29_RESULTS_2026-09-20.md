## The phase

R29 read the phase from the *change* in six-month producer-price momentum; R29B from its *level* (building above 1.0 log point a half-year, 2% a year). Czech origins by era, building / fading: R29 20/15, 9/15, 15/16; R29B 13/22, 11/13, 4/27. The change rule called January–March 2022 fading (momentum 6.6–7.2, below the 2021 peak) and February 2024 building (momentum rising from 0); the level rule has Czechia building from March 2021 to November 2022 without a break and fading from December 2022 onward except three months of 2019. The level rule is the one the specification's economics meant.

Condition 4 fails on the era-mean reading: FAST's core has a mean error of +0.02 log points at h6 and −0.00 at h12 on the full sample (it is unbiased on average, wrong in both directions), so the sign of "needed" is noise and the candidates' negative mean corrections disagree with it by construction; per-origin sign agreement is 0.74 (h6 full), 0.64 (h6 from 2024), 0.61 (h12 full) and 0.47 (h12 from 2024, 19 origins). Condition 6 fails on one of nine cells, the 2019–21 headline at h12, by 0.05% (6.617 against 6.614); the other eight improve: full h12 4.740 against 4.769, 2022–23 3.399 against 3.521, from 2024 0.602 against 0.659, the CNB pairs from 2024 0.398 against 0.405.

**Verdict: not promoted.** The declared rule has no tolerance, and I am not adding one after seeing where the candidate lands. Two amendments are declared for the next specification: condition 4's era-mean test applies only when the baseline's mean error in the cell exceeds 5% of its RMSE; condition 6 carries a 0.5% tolerance per cell. Whether the (1.0, 0.8) pair is re-judged under those amendments is the user's call, and the honest test of it is prospective, not another pass over the same 90 origins.

## What the two rounds settle

1. **The phase is real in Czech data and not established outside it.** In Czechia, holding persistence at 1.0 while producer prices run above 2% a year and pulling core toward 2% at 0.8 otherwise is better than FAST's core in every era; on the EU panel the same rule has no support before 2023 and modest support after. The mechanism the specification named (upstream pressure building or fading) shows in one cycle only.
2. **The weight matters less than the switch.** 0.6 and 0.8 differ by 3 points of era-balanced score; both pass condition 1. The switch is the phase; a symmetric pull (R21, R25, R26) fails, a switched one does not.
3. **The panel is not the way to estimate this.** Its weights are 0.35–0.60 in both phases and it puts a 0.5 pull on core at h3, which no Czech era supports.
